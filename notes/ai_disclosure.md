# AI Usage Disclosure (draft)

Required by the [Cross-AI AI Usage Policy](https://cross-ai.io/conference/2027/preconf/policies/ai-policy/#3-ai-usage-disclosure-requirements): a section titled "AI Usage Disclosure", placed as the final section before the References, counted toward the page limit. Naming the tools is optional.

## Section text (for both papers)

> **AI Usage Disclosure.** An AI coding assistant was used throughout the development of the system, for writing and refactoring code, and for discussing design options, evaluation metrics, and related literature; design decisions and evaluation of the results by listening were made by the author. The assistant also helped summarize experimental logs into lab notes. This paper was co-written with an AI assistant from the author's notes, experiment outputs, and prior writing, and the final text was co-edited by the author and the assistant. All parameters, results, and references were verified by the author against the code, the experiment records, and the cited sources. No AI tools were used to generate or process the audio; the music was produced from the network's output with conventional software instruments and effects.

## Categories covered (for the submission form, if it asks)

- Idea development and research planning: yes (design options and literature discussed with the assistant)
- Coding, programming, and system development: yes
- Experimental design, data processing, and analysis: yes (evaluation metrics were proposed in discussion with the assistant)
- Interpretation of results and data visualization: yes (plotting code; log summaries)
- Writing and text generation: yes
- Presentation and media generation: no (confirmed: no AI used for the audio; the submission has no cover art)
- Other: none

## Notes

- Idea development and experimental design are included because the lab notes record suggestions from the assistant that were adopted: the "Easy Metrics" list, the `eval_ambient` metrics, and the (μ+λ) and CMA-ES discussions. Leaving these out could read as undisclosed use.
- The audio sentence is there because reviewers of an AI music competition may assume AI was used in production. Drop it if any AI tool touched the audio.
- About 120 words, roughly 0.15 page in IEEE two-column. The short paper has room for it; the long one is already over two pages.
